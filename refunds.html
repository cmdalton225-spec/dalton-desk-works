Refund Policy

Dalton Desk Works · Last updated November 22, 2026

Fix it first

Every package includes revision rounds — 1 on Starter packages, 2 on Standard and Premium. If the wording feels off, a formula breaks, or a number looks wrong, point at it and we rework it. If a delivered file is broken or wrong, we fix or replace it.

When you get a refund

If we genuinely cannot deliver a working product, you get a full refund. The same applies if your job turns out to be bigger than the package you bought: we tell you before any work starts, and you choose an upgrade or a full refund. And if we miss the delivery date we promised, tell us — we will prioritize your order or refund it.

What we don't refund

Our work is delivered as finished digital files, which can't be returned once delivered, so we don't offer change-of-mind refunds on completed work. What we stand behind is the work itself: the deliverables listed on your package, done to the standard of the samples on this site, checked twice before delivery.

How to reach us

Email matt@daltondeskworks.com with your order number and what looks wrong. We would rather fix it than argue about it.
