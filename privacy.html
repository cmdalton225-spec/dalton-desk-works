Privacy Policy

Dalton Desk Works · Last updated November 22, 2026

What we collect

When you order, we receive your name and email address (through Payhip checkout and your requirements email), plus the files and links you send us to work on — shop or listing links, spreadsheets, and product links. That's it. This website itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.

Payments

Checkout and payment are handled by Payhip. We never see, receive, or store your card number or bank details.

How we use it

Your information and files are used only to complete your order and to communicate with you about it. We do not sell, rent, or share your information or files with anyone, and your files are never used as samples or examples.

How long we keep files

Files you send us are deleted within 30 days after your order closes. Order emails and receipts are kept for our business records.

Your choices

You can ask at any time what information we hold about you, ask us to delete it, or ask us to confirm that your files have been deleted. Email matt@daltondeskworks.com and we will take care of it.
