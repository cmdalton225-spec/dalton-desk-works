After checkout, email your file or links to matt@daltondeskworks.com with your order number: your shop or listing links for Etsy SEO, the file itself for spreadsheet work, or your product links for a pin pack. We confirm the scope in writing before work starts.

How long does it take?

Starter packages: 2 business days. Standard and Premium: 3 business days — counted from when we have your complete file or links.

Do you need my password or account login?

Never. Everything runs on links, files, and permission you control. You paste the finished copy into your own shop and post your own pins — your logins stay yours.

What if something comes back wrong?

Every package includes revision rounds (1 on Starter, 2 on Standard and Premium) — point at what is off and we rework it. If a file is broken or wrong, we fix or replace it; if we genuinely cannot deliver a working product, you get your money back.

Do you guarantee more sales or better rankings?

No — and you should not trust anyone who does. Etsy and Pinterest decide rankings. What we promise is the work itself: the deliverables listed on your package, built from real buyer search phrases, checked twice, and delivered on the date promised — and if we miss that date, tell us and we’ll prioritize your order or refund it.

My job is bigger than the top package. What then?

Email us. We will quote it honestly — or tell you plainly if we are not the right fit. Regular work can also run on a monthly retainer (below).
